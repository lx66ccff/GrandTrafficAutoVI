# Code of Conduct

We want GrandTrafficAutoVI to be a welcoming, constructive space for people with different levels of experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Assume good faith while addressing problems directly.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation in serious or repeated cases.

For now, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
